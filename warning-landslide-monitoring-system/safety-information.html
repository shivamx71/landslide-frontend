Safety Information

Guidance for residents and field teams operating in landslide-prone terrain.

🚨 Warning Signs to Watch For

  • New cracks in walls, pavement or the ground
  • Doors or windows sticking for the first time
  • Tilting trees, retaining walls, or utility poles
  • Sudden increase or decrease in stream water levels
  • Faint rumbling sound that increases in volume

🎒 Before an Event

  • Know your area's landslide and debris-flow risk
  • Prepare an emergency kit and evacuation route
  • Keep a family communication and meeting plan
  • Follow official rainfall and slope advisories

🏃 During an Event

  • Move away from the path of the slide immediately
  • Head to high, stable ground away from slopes
  • Avoid river valleys and low-lying areas
  • Stay alert — landslides can occur with little warning

🩹 After an Event

  • Stay away from the slide area — risk of further slides
  • Check for injured or trapped people near the site, without entering the direct slide path
  • Report broken utility lines to authorities
  • Watch for flooding, which may follow a landslide

👷 Field Team Protocol

  • Never inspect an active slope alone — work in pairs
  • Carry communication devices with a live GPS signal
  • Log field reports immediately through this system
  • Withdraw immediately if ground movement is felt
